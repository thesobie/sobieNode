About SOBIE
History

Founded in 1995, SOBIE was established with the aim to foster collaboration among students and professionals in the fields of business and economics.

Over the years, SOBIE has hosted numerous events, including seminars, conferences, and workshops that have contributed to the academic and professional growth of its members.

The society's commitment to providing valuable resources and opportunities has made it one of the leading organizations in its field.

Mission

The SOBIE mission is to encourage business faculty members to respond to current economic and market issues, and find practical solutions to the many problems business people and public policymakers confront.

Research Focus: SOBIE invites business and economic research, as well as interdisciplinary research.

Who Can Participate: Faculty, non-academic professionals, business people, doctoral candidates, graduate students, and undergraduate students are all invited to participate.

Students Welcome!

Free Registration: Students attend SOBIE at no cost

Student Research: Undergraduate and graduate student research is especially encouraged and welcomed

About JOBIE

SOBIE annually publishes its peer-reviewed academic journal, JOBIE (Journal of Business, Industry, & Economics).

The journal is listed in Cabell's directory, ensuring academic credibility and recognition.

JOBIE provides a platform for researchers to share their work and contribute to the advancement of business and economic knowledge.
